About Idhanushh

Idhanushh, also known as Dhanush Selvadurai, is an independent artist working across music, hip-hop, rap and pop.

His music explores themes including love, heartbreak, emotions, relationships and personal experiences.

This website is a personal encyclopedia-style archive dedicated to Idhanushh, including his music, projects, videos, gallery and updates.
