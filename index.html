Idhanushh

Dhanush Selvadurai, professionally known as Idhanushh, is an independent artist working across music, hip-hop, rap and pop.

His musical work explores love, heartbreak, relationships, emotions and personal experiences, with a focus on developing an individual sound and artistic identity.

This official personal website serves as a central place for music, projects, visual work and future announcements from Idhanushh.
